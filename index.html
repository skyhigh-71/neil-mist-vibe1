Ground rules
  1. Ride first. If the camera changes how you ride, take the camera off.
  2. Mount it like it matters. Wobbly footage gets scrolled past in a second.
  3. Shoot more angles than feels reasonable. Variety is what makes an edit look expensive.
  4. Open on the best bit. Nobody waits for the intro.
  5. Keep showing up. A steady schedule beats one huge video every six months.
