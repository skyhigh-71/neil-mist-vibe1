Five rules that matter most

  1. Ride first, film second. If recording changes how safely you ride, stop recording.
  2. Mount it solid. Shaky, loose footage is the fastest way to lose a viewer.
  3. Film more angles than you think you need. Variety is what makes a short video feel professional.
  4. Hook in the first three seconds. Open with your best moment, not a slow intro.
  5. Post consistently. A modest, steady schedule beats occasional big pushes.
New to all of this? You do not need an expensive camera. A modern phone, a secure mount and good light will out-perform a pricey setup used badly.
