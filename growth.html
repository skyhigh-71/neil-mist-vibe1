Get seen

Good riding doesn't speak for itself. You have to package it.

Pick a lane

"Mountain biking" is too wide to own. Choose a corner and be the best answer in it. You can widen later.

  • Local trails: guides and first looks at your area. Riders searching for the place find you.
  • Learning to ride: skills and beginner mistakes. Everyone starts somewhere.
  • Bike tech: setup, repairs, reviews. It gets searched for year after year.
  • Trips and bikepacking: story-led, easy to share.

Hooks, titles, thumbnails

  • First three seconds: the best moment, then explain.
  • Titles promise a result. "I learned to jump in a week" beats "Ride vlog 14".
  • A thumbnail is one idea: a clear action moment or face, strong contrast, few words.
  • Don't mislead. A cheap click costs you the viewer.

Turn up every week

Pick a schedule you can hold for a year. One long video and a couple of short clips a week is plenty. Film in batches: one good ride can feed several posts. Keep a running list of ideas, and check your numbers monthly to see what people actually watched to the end.

Don't go it alone

  • Ride with other creators and film each other.
  • Leave real comments on smaller channels, and answer your own.
  • Team up with local trail groups and bike shops.
  • Credit the builders. They're often volunteers.

The money question

Most people earn little for a long time. Over time it can come from ad revenue, sponsors, affiliate links, coaching and guided rides, or merch. None of it is guaranteed.

Disclose it

Paid, gifted or affiliate? Say so clearly. Many countries require it, and your audience will trust you more.
