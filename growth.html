Growing your audience

How to get the right people watching, and keep them coming back.

Be clear about who it is for

"Mountain biking" is broad. Content grows faster when it speaks to a specific person. Choose a lane, then widen later.

Local trails

Trail guides and first-ride previews for your area. Other riders searching for the place will find you.

Learning to ride

Skills, progression and beginner mistakes. Everyone remembers being new.

Bike tech

Setup, maintenance, reviews and upgrades. Useful, searchable and evergreen.

Adventure and travel

Trips, bikepacking and big-mountain days. Story-driven and highly shareable.

Hooks, titles and thumbnails

  • Hook in three seconds. Start on the most exciting or intriguing moment, then explain.
  • Titles promise a result. "How I Learned to Jump in One Week" beats "Riding Vlog 14".
  • Thumbnails are one idea. A clear face or action moment, strong contrast, minimal text.
  • Be honest. Misleading packaging may win a click but loses the viewer and their trust.

Consistency beats intensity

  • Choose a schedule you can keep for a year, such as one long video and a couple of short clips a week.
  • Batch your filming: one good ride can produce several pieces of content.
  • Keep a simple list of video ideas so you never stare at a blank page.
  • Look at your analytics monthly, find what held attention, and make more of it.

Collaborate and be part of the scene

  • Ride with other creators and film each other; both audiences benefit.
  • Comment genuinely on smaller creators' videos, and reply to your own comments.
  • Partner with local trail groups and bike shops. They love good local content.
  • Credit trail builders and volunteers whenever you can.

Making money, realistically

Most creators earn little at first, and that is normal. Common routes over time include platform ad revenue, sponsorship from brands, affiliate links, selling coaching or guided rides, and merchandise.

Disclose sponsorships and affiliate links Many countries require clear disclosure of paid or gifted content. Check the rules where you and your audience live, and always be upfront.

No one can promise growth. Platforms change their algorithms, so focus on making content worth watching.
