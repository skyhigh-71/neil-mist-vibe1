Editing guide

A repeatable workflow so you finish videos quickly and they hold attention.

A workflow you can repeat

  1. Back up and organise. Copy footage in, then sort into folders by angle or section.
  2. Pick your best clips. Scrub quickly and mark only the shots you love. Skip the rest.
  3. Choose the music first. Cutting to the beat is far easier than adding music afterwards.
  4. Build a rough cut. Drop clips on the timeline in story order with no polish.
  5. Tighten. Trim every clip to the part that matters. Cut anything that does not earn its place.
  6. Add polish. Speed ramps, slow motion, colour, sound effects, text.
  7. Export and review on your phone before publishing.

Pacing

  • Open with a strong clip, even if it is from the middle of the ride.
  • On fast riding, cuts between 1 and 3 seconds usually feel energetic.
  • Let scenery and slow-motion shots breathe for 3 to 6 seconds.
  • Vary angles every cut so the viewer never sees the same view twice in a row.
  • Cut on movement, such as a turn into a corner or a bike leaving the ground, for seamless transitions.

Music and sound

  • Use music you have the right to use. A copyright claim can demonetise or remove a video.
  • Royalty-free libraries and subscription music services are the safe route; read each licence.
  • Mix music below speech, and add real trail sounds (tyres, gravel, chain) at low volume for realism.
  • Duck or fade the music for talking segments, and bring it back for the descent.

Colour

  • Fix exposure and white balance first, then add style.
  • Match clips from the same ride so skin tones and greens look consistent.
  • A small contrast boost and gentle saturation is usually enough. Heavy filters date quickly.

Software to try

Pick one and learn it well. Free and low-cost editors are fully capable of professional results, and your phone's built-in editors handle quick social posts.

  • Free desktop editors with powerful colour tools.
  • Subscription editors with strong templates and stabilization.
  • Mobile editors for fast posts straight from the trailhead.

Platform specs change often. Confirm current recommendations before exporting.
